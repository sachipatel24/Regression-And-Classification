Demo

Two PyTorch models exported to ONNX and running right here in your browser. No server, no magic.

Guess the gas mileage of a car

Describe a car from the 1970s or early 1980s and the model guesses how many miles it goes on one gallon of gas. Heavy car with a big engine = drinks gas. Light car with a small engine = sips gas.

Which iris flower is it?

4 flower measurements (in cm) go in, one of 3 flower types comes out. Press a button to load a real example, or type your own.
